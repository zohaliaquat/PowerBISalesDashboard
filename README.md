# Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance across regions, product categories, and customer segments. The project was created to demonstrate practical data analysis and visualization skills using real world style retail data.

## Overview

The dashboard tracks total revenue, profit, orders, and return rate across the years 2016 and 2017. It breaks performance down by product category, continent, and customer demographics, and includes forecasting and decomposition analysis to explain what drives revenue and order volume.

## Pages

The Executive Summary page presents key performance indicators along with monthly trends for revenue, cost, and profit. It includes interactive filters for year, month, gender, and continent.

The Product Details page focuses on a single product at a time, showing weekly revenue and return trends along with a short term forecast.

The Customer Detail page ranks customers by total orders and revenue, and breaks down the customer base by gender, income level, occupation, and age.

The Maps page shows order distribution across Europe, North America, and the Pacific region on an interactive map.

The Smart Insights page uses decomposition tree visuals to compare order volume against revenue by category, subcategory, and country. This page reveals that categories with the highest order counts are not always the ones generating the most revenue.

## Key Insight

Accessories generate the highest number of orders but contribute a relatively small share of total revenue, while Bikes generate far fewer orders yet account for the largest share of revenue. This highlights the difference between order volume and business value, and shows why revenue based analysis matters alongside simple order counts.

## Tools Used

Power BI Desktop was used to build the data model, DAX measures, and all visuals in this dashboard.
